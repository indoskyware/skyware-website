About IndoSkyware

Transforming Indonesian businesses with AI-powered solutions since 2014

Our Story

Founded in 2014, IndoSkyware started as a custom software development company. Over the years, we've evolved from building traditional ERP, HRIS, POS, and CRM systems to becoming a leader in AI-powered business automation.

Our pivot to AI began in 2024 when we launched Listie AI - an AI-powered conversion engine for property agents. This marked our transformation into an AI-first company while continuing to serve our existing enterprise clients with enhanced, AI-powered versions of our classic software solutions.

Today, we proud to serve 50+ clients across Indonesia, helping them transform their businesses with intelligent automation solutions.
