Who we are

Insights

Where AI is going in the next 5–10 years.

Notes from the team building it. Our view on how artificial intelligence reshapes the software that businesses depend on.

The big shift · 9 min read

Vertical AI will quietly eat the back office.

The first wave of AI was general-purpose and horizontal. The durable value over the next decade will be vertical: models wrapped in deep domain workflow, trained on the messy reality of one industry. The winners won't be the smartest models — they'll be the ones that understand a business well enough to be trusted with its work. In accounting, HR and real estate, that means moving from software that records what happened to software that does the next thing for you.

By the Skyware team•Strategy
Agents · 6 min

The agentic enterprise: software that acts.

For thirty years business software has been a system of record. The next generation is a system of action — reconciling, ordering, scheduling and drafting, then asking a human only where judgment is required.

Automation · 2026
Product · 5 min

Why the next ERP won't have forms.

Menus and data-entry screens were a workaround for computers that couldn't understand us. As language becomes the interface, the ERP of 2032 looks less like a database and more like a colleague you brief.

Interfaces · 2026
Trust · 7 min

Trust, not intelligence, is the real moat.

Any company can call a model. Few can be trusted to let AI touch payroll, the ledger or a client's deal. The moat is proprietary data, domain judgment and accountability — exactly what years of enterprise software builds.

Governance · 2026
